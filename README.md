# Constructing multimorbidity models to predict susceptibility to COVID-19 and other complex diseases

## Project description

In this study, leveraging the nation-wide CVD-COVID-UK cohort, we will develop comorbidity risk scores (CRSs) to predict incident hospitalisation due to COVID-19, as well as a wide range of other complex diseases with high hospitalisation risk, such as myocardial infarction and lung cancer. CRSs will be constructed using the linear combination of common disease diagnoses, with interpretable predictor-specific effect sizes as weights. CRSs will be validated among different self-reported ethnicities, and the comorbidity architecture underlying the CRSs will be characterised. Overall, CRSs can potentially serve as a generalisable and interpretable risk score that may improve disease risk assessment in routine clinical settings.
